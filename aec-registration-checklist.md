# Australia United Party — AEC federal registration preparation checklist

Status: internal working checklist. Verify every item against the latest AEC guide and forms before submission.

## Non-parliamentary party pathway
- [ ] Confirm the party is eligible and intends to endorse candidates for the House of Representatives and/or Senate.
- [ ] Confirm the proposed party name is available and not prohibited or confusingly similar to an existing registered party.
- [ ] Prepare the party constitution and rules.
- [ ] Identify the party secretary, registered officer, party agent and supporting applicants as required.
- [ ] Complete the current AEC Application for registration of a non-Parliamentary party.
- [ ] Complete the current AEC party secretary appointment and statutory declaration documents, as applicable.
- [ ] Complete the current AEC party agent appointment document, as applicable.
- [ ] Decide whether to register an abbreviation and/or logo; check the current logo determination and technical requirements.
- [ ] Prepare a list of 1,500–1,650 supporting members who are enrolled on the Commonwealth Electoral Roll and are not relied upon by another party for registration.
- [ ] Match member names, residential addresses and dates of birth to the Electoral Roll. Include contact details to assist AEC testing.
- [ ] Use the AEC's current sample membership spreadsheet, not merely this application's generic export.
- [ ] Review the current fee and payment instructions on the AEC application page.
- [ ] Submit the application and supporting documents using the AEC's current instructions.
- [ ] Retain evidence of consent, source of member data, access logs and withdrawal handling.

## Important operational notes
- AEC guidance describes a 1,500-member test and asks non-parliamentary parties to submit between 1,500 and 1,650 members.
- The AEC may contact a random sample of members to confirm genuine membership.
- This app does not verify enrolment against the Electoral Roll and does not submit applications to the AEC.
- Registration timeframes and requirements can change. Confirm the current AEC guide and forms immediately before lodging.
